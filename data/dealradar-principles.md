# DealRadar product principles

1. No affiliate link = no published deal.
2. Prices must come from approved affiliate product data or another verified source that is explicitly permitted by the partner.
3. Never present demo/example data as a live deal.
4. Keep price timestamps and historical observations so deal scores can be evidence-based.
5. Affiliate disclosures must be clear near commercial links.
6. Build mobile-first and prioritize useful deal discovery over large volumes of AI-generated content.
